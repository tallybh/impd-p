# P.4 Control of Excipients

## P.4.1 Excipient Identity, Quality, and Specifications

Finished-product excipients, including the capsule shell and any additional materials introduced during Drug Product manufacturing, will be identified from the approved composition record in P.1.2. For each applicable material, the dossier will present its functional role, quality grade, applicable specification, supplier or source, and conformity documentation.

The anti-caking agent already incorporated into the final Drug Substance blend is characterized in Part S (S.3.1.4 and associated control sections). Its Drug Substance control is not duplicated as a separately introduced Drug Product excipient unless the finished-product records identify an additional use.

## P.4.2 Analytical Controls and Suitability

Where excipient quality is established by compendial or supplier-controlled specifications, the applicable documentary basis will be cited. Any additional product-specific controls or assessments that are required by the actual formulation will be identified in the corresponding records. Suitability statements will reflect the documented source, grade, and intended function of each material.

## P.4.3 Special Material Considerations

Any material-specific considerations, including noncompendial grades or biological origin where applicable, will be addressed using the actual excipient inventory and supplier declarations. No such features are presumed in advance of the confirmed formulation.
