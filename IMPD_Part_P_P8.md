# P.8 Stability

## P.8.1 Stability Summary and Conclusions

The Drug Product stability assessment will address **PSIGA2 finished capsules in their proposed final container closure system**. This assessment is separate from the **three-month Drug Substance stability objective** described in S.7 and will support any proposed finished-product storage conditions and shelf-life statement only to the extent established by capsule-specific evidence.

The stability summary will report the studied batches, package configuration, storage conditions, available observation period, quality attributes monitored, and overall interpretation of the observed results.
