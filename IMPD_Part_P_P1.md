# P.1 Description and Composition of the Drug Product

## P.1.1 Drug Product Description

The PSIGA2 Drug Product is an oral capsule containing a standardized botanical extract prepared from the fruiting bodies of *Psilocybe cubensis* (PSIGA2 production culture). The finished product is defined by a **verified content of 5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule**, as reported by PsygaBio on the basis of validated analytical testing. The associated finished-product batch records and analytical reports will be identified in Section P.5.

The Drug Substance is the dried multicomponent botanical extract, inclusive of the anti-caking agent present in the final Drug Substance blend, as defined in Part S. Its nominal psilocybin-equivalents concentration is **5% w/w, as-is**, on the basis described in S.1.5. The separate working formulation fraction of **5% w/w anti-caking agent** relates to Drug Substance composition and is documented in S.3.1.4; these two percentages represent distinct properties.

## P.1.2 Qualitative and Quantitative Composition

The Drug Product includes the defined PSIGA2 Drug Substance and a capsule shell. The identity and quantity of any additional excipients used at the Drug Product stage will be reported from the controlled finished-product composition record. The Drug Substance composition, botanical characterization, and intrinsic processing materials are documented in Part S and are not redefined here.

### P.1.2.1 PSIGA2 Drug Substance

The active component of the finished capsule is the PSIGA2 Drug Substance: the standardized, dried botanical extract blend described in Part S, including the anti-caking agent incorporated at the Drug Substance stage. It provides the declared **5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule**. The nominal concentration of psilocybin equivalents in the final Drug Substance blend is **5% w/w, as-is**. The quantitative amount included in the capsule will be reported from the controlled Drug Product formula, with the relevant Drug Substance assay documented in S.4.
