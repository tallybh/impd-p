# P.2 Pharmaceutical Development

## P.2.1 Development Basis and Product Design

The pharmaceutical development rationale will establish the relationship among the defined PSIGA2 Drug Substance, the selected oral capsule dosage form, the finished-product strength of **5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule**, and the proposed product quality attributes. Drug Substance identity, composition, assay basis, and physicochemical characterization are documented in S.1–S.4.

The finished-product formulation record will provide the development basis for the selected capsule shell and any Drug Product excipients. Supporting product-specific evaluations, where available, will be cited from the controlled development records rather than inferred from general information about botanical materials.

## P.2.2 Relationship to Manufacturing and Quality Control

The formulation and dosage-form attributes are to be connected to the control strategy described in P.3 and P.5. In particular, the finished-product strength declaration is supported by batch-specific assay evidence, and the approach to dosage-unit consistency will be defined in the Drug Product specification. The final set of quality attributes and their acceptance criteria will be recorded in P.5.1, with the related analytical methods and evidence in P.5.2–P.5.4.

## P.2.3 Container Closure and Stability Considerations

The rationale for the proposed finished-product container closure will be documented in P.7, and the suitability of the packaged capsules over the proposed storage period will be evaluated in P.8. These Drug Product assessments are distinct from the three-month Drug Substance stability objective documented in S.7.
