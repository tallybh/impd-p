# P.5 Control of the Drug Product

## P.5.1 Specification

The finished-product quality specification will be organized around the identity, declared strength, dosage-unit quality, applicable purity and microbiological attributes, and physical characteristics of the capsules. PsygaBio has designated **5 mg psilocybin equivalents per capsule** as the verified Drug Product content. The corresponding batch-specific results and validated analytical documentation will be cited in P.5.4 and P.5.3, respectively.

## P.5.2 Analytical Procedures

Finished-product analytical procedures will be presented by test purpose with method identifier, sample matrix, reported unit, and relevant controlled procedural reference. For psilocybin equivalents, the agreed reporting convention is **psilocybin + (1.39 × psilocin)** when component results are expressed on a consistent mass basis. Method details and analytical applicability to the finished capsule matrix will be documented by the relevant reports. Drug Substance methods discussed in S.4.2 will be cross-referenced where the same method is applicable to the finished product.

## P.5.5 Characterization of Impurities and Product-Related Attributes

Applicable degradation, contamination, or other quality concerns will be assessed using the Drug Product composition, available analytical findings, and the risk-based framework established for the Drug Substance in S.3.2 and S.4. The finished-product assessment will distinguish product-specific evidence from Drug Substance data and will identify any controls needed for the finished capsule matrix.

## P.5.6 Justification of Specification

The proposed finished-product specification will be justified with reference to the defined dosage form, strength, formulation, development and batch evidence, analytical method capability, and stability findings in P.8. Acceptance criteria will be presented from controlled approved specifications rather than inferred.
